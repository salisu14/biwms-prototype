# Referrer Commission Phase 5

Phase 5 reviews, approves, and prepares commissions for settlement.

Phase 5 does not pay commissions.

Phase 5 does not post commission expense or liability to the General Ledger.

## Flow

```text
Commission Ledger Accrual
→ Review Period
→ Review Batch
→ Review and Exceptions
→ Approval
→ Settlement Preparation Batch
→ Locked Settlement Snapshot
→ Future Phase 6 Payment
```

The Commission Ledger remains the authoritative financial movement record. Review and settlement-preparation records snapshot and allocate ledger entries; they do not replace or rewrite ledger history.

## Review Periods

Review periods define the date window for accrued commission review. Periods cannot overlap for the same business. Adjacent periods are allowed. Locked periods cannot be edited silently; reopening requires permission and a reason.

## Review Batches

Review batches are generated by business, period, currency, scope, and cutoff date. Batch generation is idempotent and currency-separated. The same ledger entry cannot be included in more than one review batch.

## Review Lines

Review lines snapshot commission ledger entries and source context at review time. Historical review results display from these snapshots and ledger entries, not mutable current plan configuration.

## Holds

Holds are explicit records. Releasing a hold preserves the original hold reason, actor, and timestamp. A hold reduces the settlement-eligible amount but does not edit the original Commission Ledger Entry.

Phase 5 supports full-line and amount-based holds. Operators should use amount-based holds carefully and review the resulting approved amount before submission.

## Disputes

Disputes are workflow records linked to a review line and ledger entry. Resolving a dispute never rewrites the original calculation. Upheld or partially upheld disputes create append-only commission adjustments through the existing adjustment service. Rejected disputes create no financial movement.

## Forfeiture

Forfeiture is an append-only Commission Ledger movement. It reduces outstanding commission without deleting or editing the original accrual. Partial forfeiture leaves the remaining line amount eligible; full forfeiture marks the line forfeited.

## Approval

Review batches follow:

```text
generated → submitted → approved → locked
```

Rejected batches return to review. Submitters cannot approve their own review batch.

## Settlement Preparation

Settlement preparation creates one summary line per referrer and currency, with allocation rows linking every prepared amount back to Commission Ledger Entries. Settlement batches may be submitted, approved, and locked for future payment.

Settlement preparation does not create payment records, bank transactions, vendor payments, payroll payments, or G/L entries.

## Late Reversals and Adjustments

Settlement preparation rechecks existing allocations and active holds. Reversals or adjustments after review approval but before settlement preparation reduce or change the preparable amount. Locked settlement snapshots are not silently edited; later corrections are carried forward to a later review or settlement process.

## Balances

Approval-ready balances are derived from:

- Commission Ledger Entries
- Review batch lines
- Holds and disputes
- Settlement allocations

Balances are separated by currency. Referrer records do not store an authoritative commission balance.

## Reconciliation

Run:

```bash
php artisan biwms:commission-reconcile --details
```

The command remains report-only and now includes Phase 5 review, hold, dispute, forfeiture, settlement, and allocation findings.

## Phase 6 Boundary

Phase 6 will handle commission payment, liability posting, settlement completion, and any required payment/tax remittance workflow. Those actions are intentionally outside Phase 5.
